Happy Tower - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Happy Tower ("the app", "the game") is a free casual game published by Arb Studio ("we", "us", "Arb Studio"). This policy explains what information the app accesses, collects, uses and shares.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

The game saves your best scores, selected skin, sound settings, daily gift progress and similar game progress only on your own device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising and third-party services

The app shows ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads) provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service by Google LLC. To show, measure and secure ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and operating system information, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data. This data is handled by Google under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. The only sharing of data is the advertising-related data collected by Google AdMob as described above.

Data retention and deletion

We do not store your personal data on our servers, so we have nothing to keep or delete. The game progress saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your Android settings. Data held by Google for advertising is subject to Google's own retention policies.

Age

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

Security

The app does not store personal data on our servers. Data sent to Google for advertising is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above.
